# 永远邸 / Kaguya

A Chinese personal blog containing mathematics and technology notes, personal writing, a fictional-world encyclopedia, tool bookmarks, and immersive 3D house scenes. Implemented in Astro with Svelte islands and GSAP, deployed under `/Kaguya/`.

Readers browse archives and categories, read articles, explore encyclopedia entries, and open bookmarked tools. Preserve the existing article content, configured social/legal links, search, theme switching and navigation.

Current user-pinned visual commitment: Persona 3-inspired electric blue, cyan and navy; strong creative-portfolio mask and typography motion. Both light and dark reading modes and mobile layouts remain supported. Original artwork remains the identity; do not import game logos or fabricate biographical/commercial claims.
